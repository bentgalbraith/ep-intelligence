EPI Custom Tool Development & Access Agreement

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This EPI Custom Tool Development & Access Agreement (this “Agreement”) is entered into by and between Estate Planning Intelligence LLC, a Texas limited liability company (“EPI”), and the law firm or legal organization accepting this Agreement (the “Firm”). EPI and the Firm may each be referred to as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”

This Agreement is effective as of the date the Firm accepts it (the “Effective Date”).

This Agreement governs EPI’s design and development of software tools built for the Firm and the Firm’s access to those tools. It is separate from EPI’s general program agreement. If the Firm also participates in EPI’s general program under a separate agreement, that agreement continues to govern the Firm’s access to EPI’s general program tools.

1. Custom Tool Development and Access

EPI will design, develop, host, and maintain software tools, AI-enabled workflows, document automation tools, document analysis tools, drafting-support tools, and related functionality built for the Firm’s practice (each, a “Custom Tool,” and collectively, the “Custom Tools”).

Before development of a Custom Tool begins, the Parties will agree in writing on its intended scope, functionality, and any assumptions the Parties consider material. Email confirmation is sufficient. The Parties may revise an agreed scope by mutual written agreement.

Subject to the Firm’s compliance with this Agreement and timely payment of applicable fees, EPI grants the Firm a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Custom Tools during the term of this Agreement solely for the Firm’s internal operations.

EPI will also invite the Firm to provide feedback, test in-development functionality, and participate in product and workflow discussions relating to the Custom Tools.

2. Development Process; Nature of the Custom Tools

EPI will develop one Custom Tool at a time unless the Parties agree in writing to concurrent development. A Custom Tool is “under development” from the date the Parties agree on its scope until EPI notifies the Firm in writing that it is complete.

When EPI notifies the Firm in writing that a Custom Tool is complete and available for the Firm’s ongoing use, that Custom Tool becomes a “Completed Tool.”

The Firm acknowledges that a Custom Tool under development may be experimental, incomplete, or subject to substantial change, and that development timelines are estimates rather than commitments unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

EPI may modify, update, improve, or replace a Custom Tool at any time, provided that EPI will not materially reduce the core functionality of a Completed Tool without the Firm’s consent, except where reasonably necessary for legal, security, compliance, or third-party-provider reasons. If EPI discontinues a Completed Tool, the provisions of Sections 18 and 19 apply.

3. Authorized Users

The Firm may permit its attorneys, employees, contractors, and other personnel working for or on behalf of the Firm to access and use the Custom Tools, provided that each such user is using the Custom Tools solely for the Firm’s internal business purposes and is subject to the Firm’s supervision and control.

The Firm is responsible for all access to and use of the Custom Tools by its authorized users. The Firm may not share login credentials, access links, accounts, or access to the Custom Tools with any person or entity outside the Firm.

The Firm may not resell, sublicense, distribute, rent, lease, or otherwise make the Custom Tools available to third parties.

4. Fees, Billing, and Payment

Development fee. The Firm shall pay EPI $700 per month for each month in which a Custom Tool is under development. This fee covers one Custom Tool under development at a time. Concurrent development requires the Parties’ written agreement and may be subject to an additional fee disclosed before the Firm incurs it.

Completed Tool fee. The Firm shall pay EPI $50 per month for each Completed Tool. If a Custom Tool becomes a Completed Tool during a billing period, the $50 monthly fee for that tool begins with the next billing period. The fee continues for as long as EPI provides the Firm access to that Completed Tool.

AI usage costs. The Firm shall pay the third-party AI model, inference, and document-processing costs (including optical character recognition and similar processing) attributable to the Firm’s use of the Custom Tools (“AI Usage Costs”). EPI will determine AI Usage Costs in good faith based on EPI’s usage records and applicable third-party provider pricing. EPI will provide a reasonable summary of AI Usage Costs on the Firm’s request. EPI will not apply a markup to AI Usage Costs unless EPI discloses the markup to the Firm in writing in advance.

EPI continues to absorb ordinary web hosting and infrastructure costs associated with the Firm’s use of the Custom Tools.

Billing. EPI will invoice the Firm monthly. Each invoice will include the development fee (if a Custom Tool is under development during the applicable billing period), Completed Tool fees then payable under this Section 4, and AI Usage Costs for the preceding period. The invoice itself constitutes EPI’s communication of AI Usage Costs for that period.

EPI will issue invoices through Stripe or another billing system designated by EPI. Each invoice is due within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date, unless a longer period is stated on the invoice. The Firm may pay by card, ACH, or any other payment method EPI makes available on the invoice.

Unless otherwise stated by EPI in writing, each billing period is a calendar month.

If an invoice is not paid when due, or if payment is declined, reversed, disputed through a chargeback, or otherwise not timely received, EPI may suspend or terminate the Firm’s access to the Custom Tools. EPI may also require the Firm to update its payment information before access is restored.

If no Custom Tool is under development during a monthly billing period, no development fee is payable for that period. The Firm may pause development at any time by written notice to EPI, effective as of the next monthly billing period.

5. Cancellation and Termination

This Agreement is month-to-month.

Either Party may cancel this Agreement at any time by written notice, effective at the end of the then-current billing period. If the Firm cancels, the Firm will retain access through the end of that billing period unless EPI determines that earlier termination is necessary due to breach, nonpayment, chargeback, credential sharing, security risk, unlawful use, unauthorized use, violation of confidentiality obligations, infringement of EPI’s rights, or use of the Custom Tools to build, assist, or improve a competing product or service.

Cancellation does not relieve the Firm of the obligation to pay unpaid invoices, fees for the then-current billing period, or AI Usage Costs incurred before the effective date of cancellation.

Fees paid by the Firm are not refundable if the Firm cancels, except as expressly provided in this Agreement or otherwise agreed by EPI in writing.

If EPI terminates this Agreement for any reason other than the Firm’s breach, misuse, nonpayment, chargeback, security risk, or violation of this Agreement, EPI will refund the development fee and Completed Tool fees paid by the Firm for the then-current billing period, to the extent those fees were paid and the corresponding period has not elapsed. AI Usage Costs already incurred are not refundable. EPI will process such refund to the original payment method, or by another reasonable method if that is not practicable, within 10 business days after termination.

EPI may suspend or terminate the Firm’s access immediately if EPI reasonably believes that the Firm or any authorized user has:

  • breached this Agreement;
  • failed to pay amounts owed;
  • initiated an improper chargeback or payment dispute;
  • shared credentials, accounts, access links, or access to the Custom Tools with unauthorized persons;
  • created or contributed to a security risk;
  • used the Custom Tools for unlawful, unauthorized, deceptive, abusive, harmful, or infringing purposes;
  • violated confidentiality obligations;
  • infringed or misused EPI’s intellectual property or proprietary rights;
  • used or attempted to use the Custom Tools to build, assist, or improve a competing product or service;
  • interfered with, disrupted, overloaded, or attempted to gain unauthorized access to the Custom Tools or EPI’s systems; or
  • engaged in conduct that materially interferes with EPI’s operations, systems, security, or ability to provide the Custom Tools.

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Firm’s right to access and use the Custom Tools will end, subject to Section 19.

19. Source Code Release

A “Release Event” occurs if:

  • EPI voluntarily discontinues the Firm’s access to one or more Completed Tools, or terminates this Agreement, for any reason other than the Firm’s breach, misuse, nonpayment, chargeback, security risk, or violation of this Agreement;
  • EPI ceases operations, dissolves, becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to a bankruptcy or receivership proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days; or
  • EPI becomes unable to provide or maintain the Completed Tools for more than 30 consecutive days, including as a result of the death, incapacity, or disability of EPI’s principal.

A Release Event does not include the Firm’s voluntary cancellation of this Agreement, or EPI’s suspension or termination of the Firm’s access under Section 5 for the Firm’s breach, misuse, nonpayment, chargeback, security risk, or violation of this Agreement.

Within 30 days after a Release Event, EPI (or EPI’s designated representative, successor, or legal representative) will deliver to the Firm a copy of the source code for the affected Completed Tools, together with configuration details and reasonable deployment documentation in EPI’s possession, sufficient to allow a reasonably skilled developer to deploy and operate those Completed Tools (the “Released Materials”). Delivery may be made by any reasonable secure method.

Upon a Release Event, EPI grants the Firm a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use, host, operate, modify, and maintain the Released Materials solely for the Firm’s internal business operations, including the right to engage contractors who are bound by written confidentiality obligations to do so on the Firm’s behalf.

The Firm does not acquire ownership of the Released Materials or of any EPI intellectual property. The Firm shall not resell, sublicense, distribute, publish, open-source, or otherwise commercialize the Released Materials or offer them as a product or service to any third party, and shall not use the Released Materials to develop, train, test, or assist a competing product or service. The Firm shall continue to treat the Released Materials as EPI’s Confidential Information.

The Released Materials do not include third-party software, models, APIs, services, or components that EPI is not permitted to transfer. The Firm is responsible for obtaining its own accounts, licenses, subscriptions, API keys, hosting, and third-party services necessary to operate the Released Materials, and for all costs of doing so.

The Released Materials are provided “as is,” without warranty of any kind. Following delivery, EPI has no obligation to host, support, maintain, update, or troubleshoot the Released Materials or the affected Completed Tools, and no further fees are payable by the Firm for those Completed Tools.
